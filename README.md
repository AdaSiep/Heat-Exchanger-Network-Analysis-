Introduction to Pinch Analysis and Heat Exchanger Network Design

From Process Stream Data to the First Grid Diagram

A student-oriented introduction to Pinch Analysis and Heat Exchanger Network (HEN) Design for chemical engineering students.

About

This guide explains the fundamentals of Pinch Analysis and Heat Exchanger Network Design, starting with process stream data and progressing through thermodynamic targeting and the construction of a preliminary heat exchanger network.

The accompanying Excel workbook provides a practical tool for following and exploring the calculations described in the guide.

Contents

The guide covers:

* Hot and cold process streams
* Heat-capacity flow rates
* Minimum temperature difference (ΔTmin)
* Temperature shifting
* Temperature intervals
* Heat cascades
* Pinch point identification
* Minimum hot and cold utility targets
* Pinch design rules
* Stream matching
* Stream splitting
* Grid diagrams
* Worked HEN design examples
* Network verification and energy balances

Intended Audience

This resource is intended primarily for chemical engineering students learning about process integration, Pinch Analysis, and Heat Exchanger Network Design.

Scope

The Excel workbook supports the thermodynamic targeting stage of a pinch study, including stream data, temperature shifting, temperature intervals, the heat cascade, utility targets, pinch temperatures, and a preliminary grid representation.

It is intended as an educational and validation tool, not as a full HEN synthesis or optimization program.
